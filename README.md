# Ethereum Airdrop Design Under Identity Splitting

This repository accompanies ongoing research on airdrop design and incentive mechanisms in decentralized systems.

## Overview

This project studies how pre-committed reward rules interact with strategic behavior when identity is splittable. In Ethereum airdrops, participants may split activity across multiple addresses, leading to distorted participation signals and inefficient value allocation.

The research aims to:
- Develop a formal model of airdrop participation under identity splitting
- Assess empirical patterns in on-chain data
- Provide design insights for Ethereum application-layer mechanisms

## Current Work

- Theoretical modeling of multistage reward mechanisms and strategic behavior
- Empirical analysis of decentralized platforms and participation patterns

## Planned Outputs

- Formal research paper (target: EC / WWW / AFT)
- Empirical study of airdrop participation
- Design memo for Ethereum ecosystem
